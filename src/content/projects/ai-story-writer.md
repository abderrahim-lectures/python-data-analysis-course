# AI Story Writer

## What You'll Build

- A writing tool that maintains character profiles and story beats
- Generate dialogue, suggest plot twists, and track narrative consistency
- Match writing style to your preferred genre and tone
- Support branching narrative paths for interactive fiction

## Features

- **Character Memory** — Keep character profiles, backstories, and arcs consistent
- **Plot Outline** — Maintain a structured story outline that guides generation
- **Style Matching** — Adapt to different genres, authors, and writing styles
- **Branching Paths** — Explore alternate story directions and compare outcomes

## Stretch Goals

- [ ] Add a collaborative mode where multiple writers work on the same story
- [ ] Implement world-building tools for setting and lore management
- [ ] Build export options for EPUB and PDF formatting
