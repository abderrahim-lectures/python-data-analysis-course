# Air Quality Dashboard

Breathe easier with air quality data.

## What You'll Build

- An AQI tracker fetching real-time air quality data
- Pollutant breakdown showing PM2.5, PM10, O3, and NO2 levels
- Health recommendation engine based on current conditions
- Map visualization of air quality across locations

## Features

- **AQI tracking** — Real-time air quality index monitoring
- **Pollutant levels** — Detailed breakdown of individual pollutants
- **Health advice** — Activity recommendations based on AQI ranges
- **Map visualization** — Geographic air quality heatmap display
