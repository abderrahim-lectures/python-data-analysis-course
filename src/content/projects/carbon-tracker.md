# Carbon Footprint Tracker

Measure and reduce your environmental impact.

## What You'll Build

- A travel emission calculator for flights and driving
- Diet impact assessment based on food choices
- Home energy usage tracking with emission factors
- Reduction goal setting with progress visualization

## Features

- **Travel calculator** — Estimate emissions from commuting and flights
- **Diet impact** — Score food choices by carbon intensity
- **Energy tracking** — Monitor electricity and gas consumption
- **Reduction goals** — Set targets and track monthly progress
