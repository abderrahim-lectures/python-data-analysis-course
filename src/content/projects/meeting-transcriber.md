# Meeting Transcriber & Summarizer

## What You'll Build

- Record audio from meetings and transcribe with speaker diarization
- Identify who said what with automatic speaker detection
- Extract action items, decisions, and key discussion points
- Export summaries to Notion, Google Docs, or your preferred tool

## Features

- **Speaker Detection** — Automatically identify and label different speakers
- **Action Item Extraction** — Pull out tasks and assignees from the conversation
- **Full-Text Search** — Search through past meeting transcripts instantly
- **Integrations** — Push summaries to Slack, Notion, or Google Docs

- [ ] Add real-time transcription during live meetings
- [ ] Implement sentiment analysis on meeting discussions
- [ ] Build a meeting analytics dashboard with participation metrics
