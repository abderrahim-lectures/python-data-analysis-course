# Sleep Analyzer

Understand and improve your sleep.

## What You'll Build

- A sleep log entry system with duration and quality notes
- Sleep stage estimation from basic metrics
- Quality scoring with composite sleep health index
- Trend analysis with pattern detection over weeks

## Features

- **Sleep staging** — Estimate light, deep, and REM phases
- **Quality scoring** — Composite score based on duration and continuity
- **Trend analysis** — Identify patterns across days and weeks
- **Improvement tips** — Evidence-based sleep hygiene recommendations
