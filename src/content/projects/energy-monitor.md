# Home Energy Monitor

Reduce your energy bills.

## What You'll Build

- A device-level energy consumption tracker
- Usage pattern analysis across time of day
- Cost calculations based on utility rate plans
- Savings recommendations with estimated impact

## Features

- **Device tracking** — Monitor individual appliance consumption
- **Usage patterns** — Identify peak usage and standby waste
- **Cost calculations** — Estimate bills from usage data
- **Savings tips** — Personalized recommendations to cut costs
