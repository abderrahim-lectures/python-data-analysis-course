## Why This Matters

Every later lesson uses words like *population*, *sample*, *variable* and *parameter* as if they were obvious. They are not, and the most common way to go wrong in statistics is not a wrong formula — it is answering a question about the wrong population, or treating a category as if it were a number.

This module sets the vocabulary before any arithmetic. It also sets the context: what a statistical question looks like, what kind of answer is possible, and where the numbers come from in the first place.

## What You'll Learn

- Say what statistics is for, in one sentence, and what it is not
- Use population, sample, parameter, statistic, variable and observation precisely
- Classify a variable as categorical or numeric, and name its measurement scale
- Recognise the four questions worth asking before touching any data
