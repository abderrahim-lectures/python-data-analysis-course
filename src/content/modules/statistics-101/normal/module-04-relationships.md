## Why This Matters

So far every lesson has described one column at a time. Real questions are almost always about two: does more study go with higher scores, does a higher price go with fewer sales, does a warmer day go with more ice cream. Looking at each column separately cannot answer any of these, because the answer lives in how the two move together.

This module adds the second dimension. It starts with the one summary for categorical data, then turns to the workhorse of relationships: correlation. The emphasis is on what a correlation coefficient can and cannot tell you, because its misuse is more common than its use.

## What You'll Learn

- Find the mode and explain what it is and is not good for
- Read a scatter plot before computing anything
- Compute a Pearson correlation by hand and interpret its sign and size
- Use a rank correlation when the relationship is monotone but not straight
- Explain why a strong correlation never establishes a cause
