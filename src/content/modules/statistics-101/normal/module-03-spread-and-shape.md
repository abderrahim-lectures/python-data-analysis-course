## Why This Matters

Two classes both average 70 on a test. In one, everybody scored between 65 and 75. In the other, half scored 40 and half scored 100. The mean is identical, and so is the median; the two classes are not remotely the same, and any decision based on the average alone would treat them as if they were.

**Spread** measures how far the data sits from its centre. It is the second thing you look at after the centre, and often the more important one: consistency, risk, reliability and quality control are all questions about spread rather than about averages.

## What You'll Learn

- Compute a range and say why it is a weak summary
- Compute a variance by hand, step by step, with the deviations written out
- Convert a variance to a standard deviation and explain why that is the number people report
- Use standard deviation to say how unusual a single value is
