## Why This Matters

The normal track teaches you what each measure does. The hard track makes you use them on data that does not cooperate: a real CSV with untidy types and a plausible outlier, and a pair of variables where a naive correlation would give the wrong answer.

This is the step from "I know the formula" to "I can hand in an analysis someone could act on". The standard is that every number you report comes with the check that justifies it.

## What You'll Learn

- Run an exploratory pass on a real dataset and record every decision
- Produce a small-multiples view of a distribution and defend the groupings
- Distinguish exploratory findings from confirmatory ones and label them
- Build a report whose claims are traceable to the output above them
