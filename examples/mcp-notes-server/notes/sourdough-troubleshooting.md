# Sourdough Troubleshooting

date: 2026-06-25
tags: recipe, bread, reference

Notes to myself after a rough patch of dense loaves.

## Dense, gummy crumb

Usually underproofed, not underbaked. Symptoms: doesn't spring much in the
oven, crumb looks tight and slightly wet even after cooling fully. Fix:
let bulk ferment go longer next time, especially in a cold kitchen --
timing from a recipe assumes a specific dough temperature that my kitchen
often doesn't match in winter.

## Flat, spread-out loaf

Starter probably wasn't active enough at mix time, or the dough overproofed
during bulk. Do the float test before mixing: a spoonful of starter should
float in water. If it sinks, wait and feed again.

## Great oven spring, but a pale crust

Steam escaped too early. Keep the lid on the Dutch oven for the full first
20 minutes -- resist the urge to peek.

sideways and dinner still needs to happen.
