# Apartment Hunting Checklist

date: 2026-07-05
tags: reference, checklist, life-admin

Things I forget to check when a place looks good on the listing photos.

- Water pressure -- actually run the shower, don't take the agent's word.
- Cell signal in every room, not just by the front door.
- Ask specifically about noise: nearby bars, main roads, upstairs floors.
- Check actual internet providers available at the address, not just
  "internet ready".
- Natural light at the time of day you'd actually be home -- a viewing at
  11am tells you nothing about a 7pm apartment.
- Get the true monthly total in writing: rent, building fees, parking,
  any mandatory services -- before falling for a place.
