# Weeknight Garlic Pasta

date: 2026-06-02
tags: recipe, quick, pasta

A 20-minute dinner for nights when nothing sounds good.

## Ingredients

- 200g spaghetti
- 4 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1/2 tsp chili flakes
- Parmesan, black pepper, parsley

## Steps

1. Boil the pasta in heavily salted water until just short of al dente.
2. Meanwhile, warm the olive oil over low heat and add the garlic slices.
   Cook slowly until golden, not brown -- burnt garlic turns bitter fast.
3. Add the chili flakes, then a ladle of starchy pasta water, and let it
   bubble into a light sauce.
4. Toss the drained pasta straight into the pan, adding more pasta water
   if it looks dry.
5. Finish with parmesan, black pepper, and parsley off the heat.

Worth doubling the garlic if you like it punchy.
