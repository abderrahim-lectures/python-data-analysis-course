# Book Notes: Atomic Habits (James Clear)

date: 2026-05-18
tags: books, productivity, habits

Core claim: identity change drives lasting habit change more reliably than
outcome-based goals. "I'm someone who exercises" beats "I want to lose 10kg".

## Ideas worth keeping

- The four laws of behavior change: make it obvious, make it attractive,
  make it easy, make it satisfying. Break any one of the four to break a
  bad habit; reverse them to build a good one.
- Habit stacking: attach a new habit to an existing one --
  "after I pour my morning coffee, I will write one sentence."
- The two-minute rule: shrink a new habit down until it takes less than
  two minutes to start. Momentum matters more than intensity at the start.
- Environment design beats willpower -- make good choices the path of
  least resistance (leave the guitar out, not in its case).

## Where I disagree

The book leans hard on individual willpower/environment tweaks and is
fairly quiet on habits that depend on other people (team norms, shared
routines) -- worth reading something more social-systems-focused as a
counterweight.
